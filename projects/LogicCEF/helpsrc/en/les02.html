Push button or switch?

Goal: know the difference between a push button (short signal) and a switch (lasting signal).

▶ Open this example

How it works

ComponentBehaviourEveryday example
Push buttonOn as long as you hold it down.doorbell, keyboard
SwitchStays on or off until you click again.light switch

Try it

  1. Start the simulation.
  2. Hold the push button down: the top lamp is lit. Release it: the lamp goes off.
  3. Click the switch once: the bottom lamp stays lit, even when you move the mouse away.

Exercise

Which component do you choose for a doorbell, and which one for the light in a room? Why?

Solution

A doorbell may only ring while you press: a push button. The light must stay on when you walk into the room: a switch.
