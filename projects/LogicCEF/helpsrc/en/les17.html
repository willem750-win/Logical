Driving a cart along a road

Goal: counting with LogicRun: a cart drives along a winding road and stops at the third post. Start and Finish report the beginning and the end of the road.

▶ Open this example

What do you see?

  • A switch on DIN1 (function on/off): the program on or off.
  • The LogicRun block with 6 steps.
  • On the left, in the input: three limit switches that follow the cart without a wire. Start is on when the cart is at the start, Finish when it is at the finish, and Tel (count) gives a short pulse each time the cart passes a post.
  • On the right, in the output: a cart on a winding road from START to FINISH, with 5 numbered posts. Its inputs are on the left of the block: V (forward, towards the finish) and A (backward).
  • The inputs hang on the + rail, the cart on the − rail; see Power supply.
  • The wires: Start to DIN2, Tel to DIN3, Finish to DIN4, UIT0 to V and UIT1 to A.

The program

StepOutputWait forWhat happens?
1UIT03 x DIN3The cart drives forward. After the third post it goes to the next step.
2--It stands still for 2 seconds (time 4 × 500 ms), for example to unload.
3UIT0DIN4 onIt drives on until Finish comes on.
4--Another 2 seconds still.
5UIT1DIN2 onIt drives backward until Start comes on.
6--2 seconds still; then it starts again.

How does LogicRun count?

In the wait for column, step 1 shows 3 x DIN3. Each time the cart has passed a post and Tel goes off again, LogicRun adds one. At the third pulse the step is done. In every step the counting starts again at 0. This is how a real machine measures a distance: a sensor counts the holes in a wheel, or a lift counts the floors it passes.

Try it

  1. Start the simulation and turn the switch on: the cart drives forward.
  2. Watch the posts: the lamp turns red when the cart passes, and Tel turns red for a moment too. After post 3 it stops.
  3. Open the LogicRun window (double-click the block). In step 1 you see 3 x DIN3 in the wait for column.
  4. Turn the switch off: everything stops and the cart stays where it is. Right-click the cart and choose Back to the start to put it back at the beginning.

Exercise 1: two stops

Make the cart stop at post 2 and at post 4 on the way out.

Solution

Set the condition of step 1 to 2 x DIN3. Choose step 2 and click + step twice. Make the first new step: UIT0 on, wait for 2 x DIN3 (posts 3 and 4). Give the second new step time 4, without an output: that is the second stop.

Exercise 2: stopping on the way back

On the way back the cart must also stop for 2 seconds at post 3.

Solution

Driving backward, the cart passes post 5 first, then 4 and then 3. Set the condition of step 5 to 3 x DIN3. Choose step 5 and click + step twice: a step with time 4 without an output, and a step with UIT1 that waits for DIN2 on (back to the start).

Exercise 3: how many pulses?

How many pulses does DIN3 get when the cart drives from the start to the finish in one go? And what happens if you set step 1 to 6 x DIN3?

Solution

Five: there are 5 posts along the road. With 6 x DIN3 the sixth pulse never comes: the cart drives to the finish and stays there, while LogicRun keeps waiting in step 1. When you make a machine count, check that the number is possible.
