Button bar

At the top of the window are two rows of buttons: the title bar with the general buttons, and below it the editing buttons. The editing buttons are hidden during the simulation.

Title bar

ButtonNameWhat does it do?
Panel choiceOpens a menu with a new standard panel and ready-made examples. See Panel & grid.
Open panelOpens a saved panel (.lpl) from the panels folder.
Save panelSaves the current panel as a .lpl file.
Screen copyPuts a picture of the panel on the clipboard, for example to paste into a word processor.
Start simulationSwitches between editing and simulating. During the simulation the button is red (). See Simulation.
HelpOpens this help.
InfoShows information about the program.
CloseCloses the program.

Editing buttons

ButtonNameWhat does it do?
Delete selected objectDeletes the object you selected through the right-click menu. See Working with objects.
Remove connectionsRemoves all wires from the panel. The components stay.
GridShows or hides the grid on the panel.
Guide linesTurns the helper lines on or off that appear while you drag an object.
FrameDraws a frame around object(s). See Panel & grid.
Change titleGives the panel a different title.
SettingsOpens the settings for the panel, the components and the hints. See Settings & language.

Status bar

At the bottom of the window the status bar shows information about the object under the mouse:

  1. the name or info of the object;
  2. its state or function;
  3. its X and Y position;
  4. its kind: input, processing or output.

On the far right you choose the language with the flags. See Settings & language.
