Exercises

In an exercise you get an assignment and a panel with the switches and lamps already in place. You build the circuit in between. With the Check button Logic tells you whether your circuit is right.

How it works

  1. Click an exercise below. The panel opens, and to the right of Logic the Exercise window appears with the assignment.
  2. Put the gates you need on the panel and connect everything with wires. Preferably do not move the switches and lamps: A is the top switch, B the next one, ...
  3. Click Check. Logic puts the switches in every position, just like the truth table, and compares with the right solution.
  4. If everything is right, the box turns green. Otherwise it turns red and tells you in which position of the switches a lamp or buzzer is wrong, for example: With A=1, B=1, L1 is off, but it should be on.

Closed the window? Click Exercise in the title bar to open it again. If you save the panel, it stays an exercise: you can carry on later and check again.

Making your own exercise

An exercise is an ordinary panel file (.lpl) with a few extra lines. Open it in Notepad and add at the bottom, for example:

Titel;EN;My exercise;
Opdracht;EN;Make the lamp light when A and B are on.|Use one gate.;
Verwacht;2;1;000;010;100;111;
Verboden;EXOF;
  • Titel (title) and Opdracht (assignment) can be given per language (NL, EN, FR, DE ...). A | is a new line.
  • Verwacht (expected): first the number of inputs and outputs, then the right truth table, row by row: the inputs (A, B ...) and then the outputs (L1, L2 ...), as in the truth table.
  • Verboden (forbidden, optional): gates that may not be used: EN (AND), OF (OR), NIET (NOT), NEN (NAND), NOF (NOR) or EXOF (XOR).
