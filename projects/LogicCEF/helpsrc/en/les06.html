Combining gates

Goal: use several gates in a row, and turn an everyday problem into a circuit.

▶ Open this example

The problem

A garden lamp must switch on by itself when it gets dark. You also want to be able to switch it on by hand, for example for a party during the day.

  1. “When it is dark”: the light sensor gives 1 when there is light. Dark is therefore NOT light.
  2. “… or by hand”: the output of the NOT gate and a switch go to an OR gate.
  3. The OR gate drives the lamp.

Try it

Start the simulation. Click the light sensor to switch between light and dark, and turn the switch on and off. Compare with the table.

Exercise

The neighbours complain: the lamp is on all night. It may only switch on when it is dark and someone is in the garden (a push button represents the motion detector). Change the circuit.

Solution

Replace the OR gate with an AND gate and the switch with a push button. The lamp is now only lit when it is dark (NOT light) and the motion detector sees something.
