Getting started

Five steps from an empty panel to a working circuit. The example: a lamp that only lights when two switches are on (an AND gate).

1. Choose a panel

Click Panel choice and choose Nieuw standaardpaneel (new standard panel). You get an empty panel with the component bar on the left.

2. Place components

  1. In the component bar, click Switch. The mouse pointer changes into the switch symbol.
  2. Click on the panel where the switch should go.
  3. Do the same for a second switch, an AND gate and a Lamp.

Drag a placed component with the left mouse button to move it.

3. Connect

  1. In the component bar, click Connection. The mouse pointer becomes a wire.
  2. Click the output of the first switch. A piece of wire appears.
  3. Drag the end of the wire to an input of the AND gate.
  4. Connect the second switch to the other input in the same way, and the output of the AND gate to the lamp.

More about this in Connections.

4. Simulate

Click Start simulation. The editing buttons and the component bar disappear. Click the switches: the lamp only lights when both are on. Click the simulation button again to go back to editing.

5. Save

Click Save panel and enter a name. Panels are saved as .lpl files in the panels folder. Use Open panel to load it again later.

Tip: Panel choice also offers ready-made examples: a decision panel, a counter panel and a memory panel. See Panel & grid.
