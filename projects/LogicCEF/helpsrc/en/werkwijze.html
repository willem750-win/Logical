Getting started

Five steps from an empty panel to a working circuit. The example: a lamp that only lights when two switches are on (an AND gate).

1. Choose a panel

Click Panel choice and choose New standard panel. You get an empty panel with the component bar at the top. If a button is missing there, the level may be too low: choose one with Panel choice › Level (for this example Basic is enough).

2. Place components

You place every component in the same way: click its button in the component bar (the mouse pointer takes the shape of the component) and then click on the panel where it should go.

  1. Start with the power, from the Power group: put the source at the top in the middle, the red + rail on the left and the blue − rail on the right.
  2. In the same group, click the switch and put it to the right of the + rail. Put a second switch below it.
  3. Put an AND gate in the middle of the panel.
  4. Put a lamp with two terminals (also from the Power group) to the right of the gate, a little before the − rail.
  5. The lamp will hang on a gate, so it has to be a lamp for 5 V: right-click the lamp › Lamp voltage › Lamp for 5 V (also on a gate).

Drag a placed component with the left mouse button to move it. Drag an end of a rail to make it longer.

3. Connect

  1. In the component bar, click Connection. The mouse pointer becomes a wire.
  2. Click the + terminal of the source (bottom left). A piece of wire appears. Drag its end to the line of the + rail. In the same way, connect the − terminal of the source (bottom right) to the line of the − rail.
  3. Click the line of the + rail, level with the first switch, and drag the end of the wire to the left terminal of that switch. Do the same for the second switch.
  4. Draw a wire from the right terminal of the first switch to an input of the AND gate, and a wire from the right terminal of the second switch to the other input.
  5. Draw a wire from the output of the AND gate to the left terminal of the lamp, and a wire from the right terminal of the lamp to the line of the − rail: now the circuit is closed.

More about this in Connections and in The power circuit.

4. Simulate

Click Start simulation. The editing buttons and the component bar disappear. Click the switches: the lamp only lights when both are on. Click the simulation button again to go back to editing. The same circuit is ready-made in lesson 4: The AND gate.

5. Save

Click Save panel and enter a name. Panels are saved as .lpl files in the panels folder. Use Open panel to load it again later.

Tip: Panel choice also offers ready-made examples: a decision panel, a counter panel and a memory panel. See Panel & grid.
