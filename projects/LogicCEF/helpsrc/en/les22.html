A barrier gate at a car park

Goal: open a barrier gate when someone presses the ticket button, and let it close again by itself after a few seconds. You use what you learned in lesson 20: a stopwatch and a NOT gate.

▶ Open this example

What do you see?

  • A switch on input S of the stopwatch: the car park is open.
  • A push button on R: the ticket button.
  • A stopwatch with end time 00:05.
  • A NOT gate between output Q and the barrier gate.

How does it work?

Pressing the ticket button sets the stopwatch to 00:00. While the time runs, Q is off and the output of the NOT gate is on: the barrier opens. After 5 seconds the end time is reached: Q comes on, the NOT gate goes off and the barrier closes again. The stopwatch stays at 00:05 until the next car presses the button.

This is the same idea as staircase lighting: one press of the button, and after a while the light goes off by itself.

Try it

  1. Start the simulation and switch the switch on. The stopwatch starts at 00:00, so the barrier first opens and closes after 5 seconds.
  2. Press the ticket button: the barrier goes up. The lamp on the post is amber while it moves and green when it is fully open.
  3. After 5 seconds the barrier closes again and the lamp turns red.
  4. Press the button again while the barrier is closing: it opens again at once. That way a car is never hit.

Task 1: more time

A lorry needs more time. Keep the barrier open for 10 seconds.

Solution

Stop the simulation, unlock the stopwatch and choose To 00:10 with the right mouse button.

Task 2: a green light for the driver

Light a lamp when the barrier is fully open, so the driver knows they may drive on.

Solution

Right-click the barrier and choose Place limit switches. Two limit switches appear: Closed and Open. Connect the output of the Open limit switch to a lamp.

Task 3: at the exit too

There is a second button at the exit. It must open the barrier as well.

Solution

Put a second push button and an OR gate on the panel. Connect both push buttons to the inputs of the OR gate, and the output of the OR gate to R of the stopwatch. Now each button sets the time to 00:00.
