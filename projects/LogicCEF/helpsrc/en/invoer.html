Inputs

Input components are the start of every circuit: they give a signal (on = 1, off = 0) at their output. You operate them with the mouse.

Every input component has an output on its right-hand side. That is where you start a wire to the rest of the circuit. See Connections.

If there are power rails on the panel, plug the inputs in on the right of a + rail. The voltage of that rail decides whether the input can give a signal (from 2 V).

Push button

The output is on as long as you hold the button down with the mouse. When you let go, the output goes off. Use a push button for short signals, for example to make a counter count one step.

Switch

Click the switch to turn it on or off. It stays in that position until you click again.

DIP switch

Use it to enter a number, for example to write it into a memory block.

  1. Set the number with the four small switches 8, 4, 2 and 1 (binary, 0 to 15). If 4 and 1 are on, for example, the number is 5.
  2. Click the button at the bottom right to send the number. As long as the button is pressed, the output is on and the number travels to the component at the end of the wire.

Pulse generator

Gives a pulse at regular intervals: the output keeps switching on and off, about every 0.3 seconds. Click the button at the bottom to start the generator; click it again to stop it. The lamp at the top blinks with the pulses.

Frequency: right-click the generator and choose Frequency: 0.5 Hz (a pulse every 2 s), 1 Hz, 1.7 Hz (default), 2.5 Hz or 5 Hz. The chosen frequency is shown small on the generator and is saved in the panel. You can also change it while the generator is running.

Ideal for making a counter run by itself or a lamp blink.

Light sensor

Simulates a sensor that reacts to light. Click the sensor to choose “light” or “dark”; the output follows.

Heat sensor

Simulates a temperature sensor. Click the sensor to choose “warm” or “cold”; the output follows.

Tip: give input components a clear name through the right-click menu (Change name), for example “Door open”. The name appears in the hint and in the status bar.

Limit switch

A limit switch reports where a person or a cart is. It is in the input, as with a real machine and a PLC: the machine itself is an output, its limit switches are inputs. It follows the person or the cart on the panel without a wire. Its output is on the right; connect it to an input of LogicRun.

  • You place them by right-clicking the person or the cart: Place limit switches. Logic puts one for each position, one below the other on the left of the panel. You can drag them afterwards.
  • Right-click a limit switch to choose which position it follows: S1 or S2 for the person, Start, Tel or Finish for the cart.
  • Closed and red: the position is reached and the output is on. If there is no person or cart on the panel, it shows a red ?.
  • On a panel with rails it hangs on the + rail, like a switch.
