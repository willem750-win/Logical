Welcome to Logic

With Logic you build digital circuits on a panel: switches, sensors, logic gates, counters, memories, lamps and displays, connected with wires. Then you start the simulation and watch the circuit work.

The window

  • Button bar – at the top: choose, open and save a panel, simulation, help, info and close. Below it a second row of buttons for editing the panel.
  • Component bar – on the left of the panel: choose the component you want to place.
  • Panel – the work area with grid and rulers on which you build the circuit.
  • Status bar – at the bottom: name, state, position and kind of the object under the mouse, and the language choice on the right.
Tip: hold the mouse still over a button or component for a moment: a short explanation (hint) appears. Press F1 to open the help about the component or button under the mouse.

Windows and Linux

Logic runs on Windows and on Linux. On Windows the help opens in the program's built-in browser, on Linux in your system's default browser.
