Learning path

Sixteen lessons, from a switch with a lamp to counters, memory, power supply, the relay, programming with LogicRun, controlling a motor, counting and making a person walk. Each lesson has a worked example, an explanation and an exercise to take it further yourself.

How it works: read the lesson, click Open this example and start the simulation to see the example work. Then try the exercise. Only open the solution when you are stuck.
  1. Your first circuitA switch, a wire and a lamp.
  2. Push button or switch?A short signal or a lasting signal.
  3. The NOT gateA lamp that goes out when you press.
  4. The AND gateTwo-hand control of a press.
  5. The OR gateA doorbell with two push buttons.
  6. Combining gatesA garden lamp that switches on when it is dark, or by hand.
  7. An alarmHeat sensor, panic button, buzzer and relay.
  8. CountingPulse generator, counter and 7-segment display.
  9. MemoryStoring numbers and finding them again.
  10. Power and voltagePower rails, voltmeter and why 1 V is not enough.
  11. The relaySwitching a second circuit with a push button and a switching relay.
  12. Programming with LogicRunA traffic light with a program in steps.
  13. Controlling a motorMaking a motor turn left and right with LogicRun.
  14. A packing machineCounting boxes with LogicRun and the counter.
  15. Counting in binaryLamps and a display show the same number.
  16. A person walking back and forthLimit switches tell LogicRun where the person is.
Linux: there the help opens in the normal web browser and the Open this example button does not work. Open the examples with Open panel, in the folder panels/lessen.
Tip: an example is a copy. Feel free to change it: when you Save, Logic asks for a new name, so the example itself stays unchanged.
