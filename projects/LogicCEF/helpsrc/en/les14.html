A packing machine: counting with LogicRun

Goal: count how many boxes pass by and do something else after 5 boxes, with LogicRun and the counter together.

▶ Open this example

What do you see?

  • A switch on DIN1 (function on/off): the machine on or off.
  • A push button: each press is a box passing by. The push button has two wires: to DIN2 of LogicRun and to Te of the counter. An output may have several wires.
  • The LogicRun block with three outputs: UIT0 to R of the motor (the conveyor belt), UIT1 to an orange LED (packing) and UIT2 to Re of the counter (reset to zero).
  • The counter with a display, so you see how many boxes there are.

The program

StepOnWhat happens?How long?
1UIT0the belt runswait for 5 x DIN2: until 5 boxes have passed
2UIT1the belt stops, the LED is lit: packing4 x 500 ms = 2 seconds
3UIT2the counter goes back to 01 x 500 ms

Step 1 has no fixed time: the wait for column shows 5 x DIN2. The program counts the pulses on DIN2 and only continues at the fifth (see Waiting). At the top of the window and at the bottom of the block you see how many have been counted, for example DIN2 4/5.

Try it

  1. Start the simulation and switch the switch on: the belt runs.
  2. Press the push button five times. The display counts along.
  3. After the fifth box the belt stops and the LED is lit for 2 seconds. Then the counter goes back to 0 and the belt runs again.

Exercise

Make boxes of 3 instead of 5. Then make the belt only start again when someone presses a second push button ready.

Solution

In step 1 click 5 x DIN2, choose Until number of pulses on input › DIN2 and enter 3. Add a step after step 3 without outputs and choose in wait for: Until input on › DIN3. Put a second push button on the panel and connect it to DIN3. Leave DIN3 on - in the input functions.
