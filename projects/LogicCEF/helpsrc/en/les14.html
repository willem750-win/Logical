A packing machine: counting with LogicRun

Goal: count how many boxes pass by and do something else after 5 boxes, with LogicRun and the counter together.

▶ Open this example

What do you see?

  • A switch on DIN1 (function on/off): the machine on or off.
  • A pulse generator: each pulse is a box passing by, all by itself, without pressing anything. The generator has two wires: to DIN2 of LogicRun and to Te of the counter. An output may have several wires.
  • The LogicRun block with three outputs: UIT0 to R of the motor (the conveyor belt), UIT1 to an orange LED (packing) and UIT2 to Re of the counter (reset to zero).
  • The counter with a display, so you see how many boxes there are.

The program

StepOnWhat happens?How long?
1UIT0the belt runswait for 5 x DIN2: until 5 boxes have passed
2UIT1 + UIT2the belt stops, the LED is lit (packing) and the counter is at 04 x 500 ms = 2 seconds

Step 1 has no fixed time: the wait for column shows 5 x DIN2. The program counts the pulses on DIN2 and continues as soon as the fifth pulse is over (see Waiting). At the top of the window and at the bottom of the block you see how many have been counted, for example DIN2 4/5.

In step 2, Re of the counter is high all the time. As long as Re is high, the counter stays at 0: the pulses the generator gives in the meantime are not counted. So the counter neatly starts at 1 again with the next box.

Try it

  1. Start the simulation and switch the switch on: the belt runs.
  2. Click the small button under the pulse generator: the boxes arrive. The display counts along.
  3. After the fifth box the belt stops, the LED is lit for 2 seconds and the display shows 0. Then the belt runs again and counting starts over.
  4. Switch the switch off: the machine stops.

Exercise

Make boxes of 3 instead of 5. Then make the belt only start again when someone presses a push button ready.

Solution

In step 1 click 5 x DIN2, choose Until number of pulses on input › DIN2 and enter 3. Add a step after step 2 with only UIT2 on (so the counter stays at 0) and choose in wait for: Until input on › DIN3. Put a push button on the panel and connect it to DIN3. Leave DIN3 on - in the input functions.
