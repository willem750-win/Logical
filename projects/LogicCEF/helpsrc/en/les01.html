Your first circuit

Goal: connect a switch to a lamp with a wire, and make the circuit work with the simulation.

▶ Open this example

Build it yourself

  1. Choose Panel choice › New standard panel and delete what you do not need, or start with an empty panel.
  2. In the component bar, click the switch and click on the left of the panel.
  3. Click the lamp and place it on the right of the panel.
  4. Click Connection. Draw a wire from the output of the switch to the input of the lamp. See Connections.

Try it

  1. Click Start simulation.
  2. Click the switch: the lamp goes on. Click again: the lamp goes off.

A wire carries a signal: on (1) or off (0). A red wire means “on”. Where the plus and the minus are is explained in Where are the plus and the minus?

Exercise

Make the same switch turn two lamps on and off at the same time.

Solution

Put a second lamp on the panel and draw a second wire, also from the output of the switch. Several wires may start from one output: each wire carries the same signal.
