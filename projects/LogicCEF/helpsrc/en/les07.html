An alarm

Goal: use sensors and send one signal to several output components.

▶ Open this example

How it works

  • An NTC with a 1 kΩ resistor is the heat detector: the warmer, the smaller the NTC and the more voltage to the gate. Above about 42 °C that is a 1. A push button is the panic button.
  • An OR gate: the alarm goes off when the sensor or the panic button reports something.
  • Two wires leave the output of the OR gate: to the buzzer (the sound) and to a relay. With a relay a small control signal can switch a large device, for example a siren or the lighting.

Try it

Start the simulation. Drag the slider of the NTC to cold and warm, and press the panic button. Watch the relay: the LED turns green and the contact switches over.

Exercise

Add an LDR with a resistor as a burglar detector (as in lesson 6): the alarm must also go off when a light comes on in the warehouse at night. The OR gate has only two inputs.

Solution

Put a second OR gate between the first OR gate and the outputs. The first OR gate combines heat and panic button, the second adds the LDR. Buzzer and relay are connected to the output of the second gate.
