Counting to 1000 with the step counter

Goal: count pulses in ordinary numbers, up to 10, 100 or 1000, and switch on a lamp when the goal is reached. Then count how often the person from lesson 16 walks back and forth.

▶ Open this example

What do you see?

  • A pulse generator on input T of the step counter: every pulse counts one.
  • A push button on R: sets the counter back to 0.
  • The step counter with a four-digit display. At the bottom you see the end value: to 10.
  • A lamp on output Q: it switches on when the end value is reached.

Counter or step counter?

The counter block from lesson 8 counts in binary, from 0 to 15, and needs a separate display. The step counter counts in ordinary (decimal) numbers up to 9999 and shows the count itself. With output Q the rest of the circuit knows when it is enough: after 10 boxes, 100 steps or 1000 laps.

Try it

  1. Start the simulation and click the button at the bottom of the pulse generator: the step counter counts 1, 2, 3 ...
  2. At 10 the lamp switches on and the counter stops: extra pulses no longer count.
  3. Press the push button: the counter is back at 0, the lamp goes off and counting starts again.
  4. Keep the push button pressed: while R is on, the counter does not count.

Also look at the status bar at the bottom: with the mouse on the step counter you read the count and the end value.

Task 1: up to 1000

Make the lamp switch on only after 1000 pulses. How can it go faster?

Solution

Stop the simulation, unlock the step counter (right-click, Unlock) and choose To 1000 with the right mouse button. To count faster, right-click the pulse generator and choose 5 Hz under Frequency. It still takes over three minutes: 1000 pulses of 0.2 seconds each.

Task 2: again and again

Make the counter always count from 1 to 10, without the push button. What does the lamp do?

Solution

Right-click the step counter and switch on Repeat. After 10 the next pulse starts again at 1. The lamp is on for one pulse, at every tenth pulse: a signal every ten.

Task 3: counting the person's steps

Open the example of lesson 16 and count how often the person arrives on the right. After 5 times a lamp must be on.

Solution

Put a second limit switch in the input area and choose S2 (right) with the right mouse button. Connect a step counter with end value 5 behind it, and a lamp on Q. Start the simulation and switch the switch on: every time the person arrives on the right, the step counter adds one.
