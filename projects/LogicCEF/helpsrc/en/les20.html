Switching on time with the stopwatch

Goal: measure time with a stopwatch and switch a lamp or motor when a set time has passed, like a kitchen timer or staircase lighting.

▶ Open this example

What do you see?

  • A switch on input S of the stopwatch: while it is on, the time runs.
  • A push button on R: sets the time back to 00:00.
  • The stopwatch with a display in minutes and seconds (MM:SS). At the bottom you see the end time: to 00:10.
  • A lamp on output Q: it switches on when the end time is reached.

Counting or measuring time?

The step counter from lesson 19 counts pulses: how fast they come does not matter. The stopwatch measures how long input S is on. That is why it needs no pulse generator: an ordinary switch is enough.

Try it

  1. Start the simulation and switch the switch on: the time runs and the colon blinks.
  2. Switch it off for a moment: the time stops (pause). Switch it on again: the time continues.
  3. At 00:10 the lamp switches on and the stopwatch stops.
  4. Press the push button: the time is back at 00:00 and the lamp goes off.

Also look at the status bar at the bottom: with the mouse on the stopwatch you read the time and the end time.

Task 1: after one minute

Make the lamp switch on only after 1 minute. And after 2 minutes and 30 seconds?

Solution

Stop the simulation, unlock the stopwatch (right-click, Unlock) and choose To 01:00 with the right mouse button. For 2 minutes and 30 seconds choose Other end time... and type 02:30.

Task 2: counting minutes

Count how many minutes the switch has been on, with a stopwatch and a step counter.

Solution

Choose To 01:00 on the stopwatch and switch on Repeat. After every minute it starts again at 00:01 and Q gives a signal of one second. Connect Q to input T of a step counter: it now counts the minutes.

Task 3: a motor that stops

Make a motor run until the time is up. After that it must stand still.

Solution

Q only comes on when the time is up; the motor must do exactly the opposite. So put a NOT gate between Q and input L of the motor. While the time runs, Q is off and the output of the NOT gate is on: the motor runs. At the end time Q comes on and the motor stops.
