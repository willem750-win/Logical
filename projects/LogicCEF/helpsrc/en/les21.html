A traffic light with pedestrian button

Goal: control a traffic light with LogicRun like in the street: the cars have green until a pedestrian presses the button, then the pedestrian gets green.

▶ Open this example

What do you see?

  • A switch on DIN1: switches the program on and off.
  • A push button on DIN2: the pedestrian button.
  • The LogicRun block with the program Traffic light with pedestrian button.
  • The traffic light on UIT0 to UIT4: red, amber and green for the cars, and a red and a green figure for the pedestrians.

The program

StepCarsPedestriansHow long?
1greenreduntil the button on DIN2 is pressed
2amberred3 seconds
3redred1 second: the crossing is empty
4redgreen8 seconds: the pedestrian crosses
5redred2 seconds, then step 1 again

Step 1 waits for an input: you choose that in the LogicRun window in the column wait for (Until input on, DIN2). The other steps last a fixed time: 6, 2, 16 and 4 times 500 ms.

Try it

  1. Start the simulation and switch the switch on: the cars get green, the pedestrians red.
  2. Press the push button: the light changes to amber, then red, and the green figure comes on.
  3. After 8 seconds everything is red again and the cars get green again.
  4. If you do not press the button, it stays green for the cars.

Task 1: more time to cross

Older people need more time. Give the pedestrians 12 seconds of green.

Solution

Double-click the LogicRun block. Set the time of step 4 to 24 (24 x 500 ms = 12 seconds).

Task 2: blinking green

In many places the green figure blinks shortly before the end. Make the green figure blink 3 times before it turns red.

Solution

Make step 4 shorter (for example 10) and add six short steps after it that switch the green figure on and off in turn: red for the cars with the green figure (UIT0 + UIT4), then only red for the cars (UIT0), each step 1 time 500 ms. Then the step with everything red follows.

Task 3: danger!

In one step, switch on green for the cars and green for the pedestrians at the same time by mistake. What does the traffic light do?

Solution

The traffic light gets a red border with a ! and the status bar says DANGER. At a real crossing that must never happen; that is why a real traffic light always has a few seconds of all red in between.
