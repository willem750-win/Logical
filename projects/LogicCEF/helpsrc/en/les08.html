Counting

Goal: count pulses with a counter, show the count on a display, and understand binary counting.

▶ Open this example

How it works

  • The pulse generator gives a pulse every 0.3 seconds on the count input Te of the counter.
  • The push button on Re resets the counter to 0.
  • The red output on the right goes to the 7-segment display: it shows the count as a digit, and after 9 as A to F.

Binary counting

Below the counter there are four small lamps: 8, 4, 2 and 1. Add up the numbers of the lamps that are lit, and you have the count:

8421CountDisplay
00112 + 1 = 33
01104 + 2 = 66
10108 + 2 = 10A
111115F

After 15 the counter starts again at 0.

Try it

  1. Start the simulation and click the button at the bottom of the pulse generator: the counter starts running.
  2. Click again to stop, and check the count with the lamps 8-4-2-1.
  3. Press the push button: the counter is back at 0.

Exercise

Replace the pulse generator with a push button, so you give the pulses yourself. How many times do you have to press to get C on the display?

Solution

C is 12 (A = 10, B = 11, C = 12): press twelve times. In binary that is 8 + 4, so the lamps 8 and 4 are lit.
