Settings & language

With Settings you change the appearance of the panel and the components. The settings are saved and also apply the next time you start the program.

The tabs

General
Settings of the logic panel, such as grid, colours and rulers.
Switch Elements
Settings of the component bar: which components it contains and how they look.
Hints
The texts of the hints in the component bar.

Changing a setting

  1. In the list, tick the box of the property you want to change.
  2. Click the value and choose or type the new value.

Click info in the list for a short explanation of the tab.
