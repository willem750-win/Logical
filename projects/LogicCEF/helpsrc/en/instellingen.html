Settings & language

With Settings you change the appearance of the panel and of the component bar. The settings window appears in the middle of the panel and has three tabs: General, Switch Elements and Hints.

Changing a setting

  1. In the list, tick the box of the property you want to change.
  2. Click the value and choose or type the new value. For a yes/no choice, True means yes and False means no.

You see the change right away on the panel or in the component bar. When you close the settings window, the settings are saved (see Saving the settings).

Switch Elements tab

Here you choose for each component whether its button is in the component bar: True shows it, False hides it. Connection is the button for laying wires.

  • Power rails (+ and -): the two buttons for the power rails at the bottom of the component bar.
  • Voltmeter: the button of the voltmeter.

Schematic symbols

With Schematic symbols (KSymbolen) you choose how the components are drawn, on the panel and in the component bar. Choose what your school uses; everything works the same in every style and you click in the same place. You can also choose it quickly with the list at the top in the title bar, left of the Info button.

  • Afbeelding (picture, default): the components as pictures, the gates as rectangles with AND, OR or NOT.
  • IEC (European standard): schematic symbols as in European textbooks.
  • ANSI (American standard): like IEC, but with the American shapes for the gates and the lamp.

Tip: This way you give students only the components they need for an exercise.

Hints tab

For each component, the text of the hint that appears when you hold the mouse over its button in the component bar. Show hint (ShowHint) turns these hints on or off.

The hint texts follow the chosen language. If you choose another language or restart Logic, the default texts of that language come back. Your own text is therefore temporary.

Saving the settings

When you close the settings window, Logic automatically saves the settings in the ini folder (next to the program). They apply again at the next start.
